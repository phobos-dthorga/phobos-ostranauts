# Phobos Spacer Stories provenance

Phobos Spacer Stories is original Phobos writing under the repository licence: company
histories, news, adverts, crew talk, loading lore, encyclopedia articles, letters and
archive documents around vanilla station life and the fictional makers of Phobos equipment. It was drafted with
OpenAI's ChatGPT at the owner's request on 6 October 2026 and reviewed for the project;
see the authoring record (docs/development/spacer-stories-authoring.md).

It contains no game text, asset, definition or code. Blue Bottle Games' Ostranauts
1.0.1.5 data (tips, headlines, adverts and the encyclopedia) were read locally only to
keep the writing consistent with the game's setting; nothing from them is copied. The
companies and incidents are original fiction, not the game's official history, and
imply no endorsement by Blue Bottle Games, Kitfox Games or any organisation.

The add-on is data only. Phobos Framework, a separately supplied dependency, reads its
story files; the game's own data cards carry its archive documents.

The vanilla-world volume records thematic influences from Ursula K. Le Guin,
C. J. Cherryh and William Gibson in the
[expansion research record](../../docs/development/spacer-stories-vanilla-expansion.md),
with primary author/estate and publisher links. No prose, characters, fictional
settings or technology from their works are adapted. These influences do not imply
author, estate or publisher endorsement, or change the licence of the original
Phobos text. Promotional artwork provenance remains separate from literary and
game-setting sources.
