# Phobos Manufacturing provenance

The 0.0.1 scaffold is original Phobos code under the repository licence. It uses
Phobos Framework as a separately supplied dependency; Framework retains its own
upstream adaptation notices. Manufacturing copies no OCF or Salvage Workshop
code, definitions or artwork and requires neither mod.

Native repair, tools and stock evidence comes from locally inspected
[Blue Bottle Games' Ostranauts](https://store.steampowered.com/developer/bluebottlegames/).
The local audit outputs and game assemblies are excluded from distribution.
BepInEx and Unity references are resolved from the owner's installation and are
not bundled. No game assets or decompiled game source are included.

Research attribution, dates, links and limits are beside the supported claims in
`manufacturing-research.md` in the package (`docs/manufacturing-research.md` in
the repository). NASA, ESA, Made In Space, Tormach and Sandvik Coromant are
sources, not endorsers of this fictional equipment or its gameplay balance.

No raster artwork has been generated, commissioned or licensed for this scaffold.
Keep future provider terms, prompts, source hashes and approval records in
`assets/phobos-manufacturing/`, separately from scientific attribution. Do not
infer artwork redistribution permission from a research citation.
