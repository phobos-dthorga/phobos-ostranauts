# Phobos Manufacturing provenance

Manufacturing is original Phobos code under the repository licence. It uses
Phobos Framework as a separately supplied dependency; Framework retains its own
upstream adaptation notices. Manufacturing copies no code, definitions or artwork
from Rusty150's Ostranauts Crafting Framework or Salvage Workshop (both MIT,
inspected locally on 29 September 2026 for inspiration only) and requires neither.

Native item, gas, explosion, mining-table and trade evidence comes from locally
inspected [Blue Bottle Games' Ostranauts](https://store.steampowered.com/developer/bluebottlegames/)
1.0.1.5. The clay hydrates chunk reuses the game's hydrate mineral artwork at
runtime by reference; no game asset is copied into the package. Game assemblies,
decompiled source and audit outputs are excluded from distribution. BepInEx and
Unity references are resolved from the owner's installation and are not bundled.

Research attribution, dates, links and limits are beside the supported claims in
`manufacturing-refinery-and-chemistry.md` in the package
(`docs/development/manufacturing-refinery-and-chemistry.md` in the repository)
and, for the machining proposal, `manufacturing-research.md`. NASA, NIST, the
OSIRIS-REx science team, ESA, Made In Space, Tormach and Sandvik Coromant are
sources, not endorsers of this fictional equipment or its gameplay balance.

Artwork: the seven Fennmark sprites were generated with PixelLab (subscription
allowance, no purchases) from original Phobos text and start drawings, under
PixelLab's terms of service as checked on the generation date. Prompts, seeds,
job IDs, costs, hashes and reviews are in `assets/artwork-completion/manufacturing-requests.json`
and the manifest; see `manufacturing-art-brief.md` in the package. Provider
terms are separate from code licensing and from scientific attribution.
