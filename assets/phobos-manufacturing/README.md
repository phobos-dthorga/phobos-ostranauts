# Manufacturing artwork brief and provenance boundary

25 September 2026. **Planning only. No raster assets generated or approved.**
The working proposal is Phobos' Rivetline M4 Enclosed Machining Centre; M4 is a
provisional model designation. Its source-backed design is in the repository's
`docs/manufacturing-research.md` and the prepared package's
`manufacturing-research.md`. These notes are packaged as `manufacturing-art-brief.md`.

## First useful pilot

After fixture/footprint registration is settled, review one representative
overhead base-plus-workpiece composite before expanding the state family.
The owner's updated 25 September policy permits a ChatGPT high-resolution
enclosure master when useful, with separate PixelLab workpieces, fittings and
state sprites. PixelLab remains preferred for simpler layers; neither provider
nor a dual-provider pass is mandatory. Follow `docs/asset-generation-policy.md`
and the Agriculture layering precedent, without importing its artwork.

Read current capability/allowance/cost before submitting a generation. Research
does not initiate paid generation or authorize purchasing extra credit. Do not
switch to a costlier provider merely because the selected allowance is unavailable.

The 4 × 4-tile machine uses a proposed **64 × 64 native world derivative** and
at least **128 × 128 production master**. Keep larger originals. Portraits at
256 × 256 need a 512 × 512 master. Do not enlarge the world footprint by shipping
a larger PNG at the old native scale. Use nearest-neighbour integer previews.

Silhouette: compact industrial enclosure, restrained window/work opening,
positive fixture jaws, a short spindle head, cartridge access and one readable
power point. Coarse deliberate clusters, utilitarian muted colour and clear
forms should read at 64 × 64. Native conduit remains separately placed ship
infrastructure, not painted integral loops. No institution logos or baked text.

## Registered source layers

Keep one canvas, centre/pivot, front facing local -Y and unchanged transparent
margins across these original layers:

- Enclosure/base and service door, independent of the selected recipe.
- Spindle/fixture and open/closed access state.
- Stock carrier, twin preform and separated-parts overlays.
- Foreground guard/lip occlusion so workpieces sit inside the enclosure.
- Captured material/cartridge state, subordinate to the machine silhouette.
- Registered damage overlays and colour/normal maps.

Authoring layers do not require separate game objects. Flatten static variants
and use the native visual path only for required runtime state changes, sharing
rotation, lighting, visibility and damage. Inspect palette, projection and pixel
density across the composite; resampling alone cannot reconcile mismatched styles.

Registration becomes authoritative only when real masters exist. Avoid an empty
export framework or invented hashes/seeds. Future flattened exports go under
`mods/PhobosManufacturing/images/phobos/manufacturing/`; add that directory with
the first genuine export and a repeatable exporter using existing conventions.

Item proposals: 32 × 32 preform with at least 128 × 128 master; 16 × 16 sink
and cartridge forms with at least 64 × 64 masters. A spent kit may need a larger
inventory size than the fresh kit; settle this before export. Do not infer item
capacity from its picture. Loose/installed/damaged equipment stays aligned.

## Separate rights and research records

Retain untouched output, exact prompt, seed if supplied, provider/model/operation,
job ID, cost, dimensions, hashes, references, export settings and review state
with each generated asset. `source/` holds original masters; `previews/` holds
native-scale/integer-enlarged reviews. Future source subdirectories follow real
layer needs, not hypothetical equipment families.

Research attribution belongs beside design claims. Art provenance/licensing is
a separate record, including current provider terms checked at generation time.
No web photos, manufacturer CAD, extracted game artwork or foreign mod sprites
are redistributed or submitted as generation inputs. Original shape references
do not imply NASA, ESA, Made In Space or manufacturer endorsement.

Reuse vanilla UI through existing Framework runtime adapters with localized
live labels. No generated control-panel texture is needed for this slice.
