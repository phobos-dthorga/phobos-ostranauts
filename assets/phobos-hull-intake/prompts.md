## Exterior grabber v1

Output: `source/PhobosExteriorGrabber-concept-v1.png`

Image inputs: none; new image from the following exact prompt.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original static overhead pixel-art EXTERIOR SALVAGE GRABBER concept for the Phobos Ostranauts mod. One isolated machine component, not a ship or a scene.
Primary request: a compact exterior industrial salvage intake FOUR tiles wide left-to-right and THREE tiles deep top-to-bottom. It will adjoin a separate four-tile-wide, one-tile-deep hull transfer chute along its bottom/rear edge. Show only the grabber here, not the chute. Intended eventual game sprite is 64 x 48 pixels. Use a horizontal 4:3 overall bounding rectangle.
View/layout: strict orthographic overhead, space and approaching material at TOP, ship attachment edge at BOTTOM. The clear silhouette is a squat U-shaped industrial capture cradle: TWO chunky short clamp arms, one left and one right, reaching upward from a strong rear crossbeam and bending their jaw tips slightly inward. Exactly one arm on each side. Each arm has one roundish stepped grey pivot, one blocky enclosed actuator, a blue structural link and one ochre gripping pad. Keep both arms and their tips wholly inside the 4 by 3 footprint. Open central dark catch bed with two broad retaining rails, EMPTY, no cargo, clear enough to read the arms independently. A small welder-like laser cutting head is mounted centrally on the lower rear crossmember BETWEEN the arms, pointing upward into the capture zone; compact pale grey rectangular head with a dark nozzle and one subdued red tip, entirely inactive, no beam or sparks. The head should occupy less than one quarter of the width. It must not block the full material route: short central projection only, ample dark space ahead.
Supporting parts: one modest cream maintenance cover at the lower-left rear, a small dark electronics cover at lower-right, one capped electrical socket at the rear. Muted blue rectangular crossbeam across the bottom; broad flat joining edge for the separate hull chute. Detail must be sparse and functional: arms first, cutting head second, small service parts last. Leave significant dark/transparent negative space in and around the open jaws.
Style: visibly coarse low-resolution ship-machinery pixel art, as if nearest-neighbour enlarged from an actual 64 x 48 drawing. Large hard square pixel clusters, stepped silhouettes, only two or three flat tones per material, subtle edge highlights without smooth gradients. Muted industrial blue frame, cream service cover, charcoal tray, dull steel joints and rails, tiny ochre grip/safety accents. Match an existing companion 4x4 feeder with these colours, not a futuristic polished robot. Strong shapes and contrast instead of intricate greebles.
Composition: complete object in a 4:3 landscape canvas with minimal equal margin, perfectly overhead. Genuine transparent background, opaque machine surfaces. No wall, floor, scenery, ship, human, cable network, external conduit, gas pipes, additional machinery, labels, text, dimensions, logos or watermark. No photorealism, isometric sidewalls, blur, metallic gradients, dense scratches, surface noise, animated frames, glow or cast shadow. Original artwork, no copying of an existing game sprite.
```

## Hull chute v1

Output: `source/PhobosHullChute-concept-v1.png`

Image inputs: none; new image from the following exact prompt.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original overhead pixel-art hull transfer chute concept for the Phobos Ostranauts mod. One isolated static machine component, not a scene.
Primary request: a shallow SEALED HULL TRANSFER COLLAR / CHUTE occupying FOUR tiles left-to-right along a hull and ONE tile top-to-bottom across it. The machinery artwork has exactly a 4:1 width-to-depth silhouette, intended eventually for a 64 x 16 pixel sprite. It connects an exterior salvage grabber to an interior industrial panel feeder, whose family uses muted industrial blue frames, cream service covers, charcoal working surfaces, dull grey rails and small ochre clamp tips.
View/layout: strictly overhead orthographic. Exterior side is at image top; interior side at bottom. Wide horizontal rectangular frame, reinforced squat mounting shoe at each end, narrow muted blue outer ribs, dark central transfer throat with a CLOSED grey segmented safety shutter, two broad transverse shutter seams, pale cream seal covers along the top and bottom edges, one compact dark actuator at each end. The shutter face should read as a sealed hatch from above, not a view through an open hole. Four chunky fastening marks total and two short ochre corner safety marks are sufficient. One capped unplugged power connection on an end shoe. Entire component remains within its shallow 4:1 envelope, no protruding arm or long belt.
Style: deliberately coarse game pixel art, visible hard square pixel clusters as if nearest-neighbour enlarged from a 64 by 16 pixel drawing. Simple flat two or three tones per material; angular stepped corners; only slight edge relief. Match restrained utilitarian low-resolution ship machinery. Do not add small intricate greebles. No gradients, fine noise, glossy photoreal metal, soft painted shading, smooth curves, perspective or isometric sides.
Composition: place this single complete 4:1 horizontal object centered on a square transparent canvas, occupying about 92 percent of the canvas width and 23 percent of its height. Large transparent regions above and below are intentional. Genuine alpha background, opaque material, no checkerboard painted into the image. No ship wall or floor supplied, no scenery or other machines, no cargo, no glow, no cast shadow, no conduit wires or pipe loops, no text, labels, logos, symbols or watermark. No dimensions or title inside the image. Original companion artwork; do not reproduce any existing game sprite.
```
