# Rejected artwork archive

## Banking cover refinement, 7 October 2026

The first Phobos Banking scene, generated by ElevenLabs Creative Studio, was
superseded when the owner requested a new OpenAI scene in the game's overhead
pixel-art style. Its unchanged master was verified byte for byte in commit
0def38b006d203ee7c733c7f65e444ce1628cb7b on codex/rejected-artwork. The owner
confirmed commercial licensing for that generation; licensing was not the reason
for replacement. The original prompt, provider record, reason and hash are retained
in [the Banking composition manifest](workshop/composed.json) and the machine-readable
inventory below. The [archived image](https://github.com/phobos-dthorga/phobos-ostranauts/blob/0def38b006d203ee7c733c7f65e444ce1628cb7b/assets/workshop/sources/PhobosBank-elevenlabs-rejected.png)
is pinned to that commit.

## Support machinery follow-up, 5 October 2026

Commit `bccf43af5b5e215cfeca10e1d24d869bfc5e1c46` preserves the three
superseded L2/A2/Corker-2 working masters, 26 unselected generated originals and
31 redundant reference copies. All 60 files were checked byte for byte before
removal from main. It was pushed on 5 October 2026; existing archive commits
remain intact. The owner narrowed the task to leave really small sprites with
PixelLab, so the small-item, one-tile and floor trials were never exported.
Requests, costs, provider jobs, the clarification and exact archive identities
remain in [the support-machinery record](phobos-manufacturing/manufacturing-restyle-requests.json).
The three selected sources, working masters, required references and runtime
exports remain on main. Existing silos and small runtime sprites are unchanged.

## Chemical machinery revision, 5 October 2026

Seven previously selected working masters (V4, X2, K2, AX-2, LC-3, SA-3 and
Copperhead-3) were verified byte for byte in commit
`4b2c15852cdd78e02b61abe328e1b9dd8d173825` on `codex/rejected-artwork` before
removal from main. The commit was pushed on 5 October 2026. Their original PixelLab
records and hashes remain in the [replacement provenance](phobos-manufacturing/chemical-reactor-requests.json).
Earlier native sprites remain required generation/review inputs on main; new
sources and selected working masters are retained for the owner-requested restyle.
All silos/tanks and other support art are unchanged. [archive-artwork.py](../scripts/archive-artwork.py)
provides the repeatable isolated-index archive route, checks selected-source and
path guards, verifies exact stored bytes, then removes only the named assets.
