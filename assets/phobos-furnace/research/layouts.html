Proposed installation

  • Short heat connection. Coupler touches the furnace in this arrangement. No shared ship-wide heat network.
  • Finite radiator. Candidate 12 m² effective emitting area after surface/view losses; fin geometry still needs validation.
  • Hull stays sealed. Cooling fittings retain backing walls as the native pressure barrier.
  • Power is separate. Ordinary conduit supplies controls and pumps; it is not part of any machine sprite.
  • Access stays clear. Leave the loading edge and native reactor service clearances usable.

Direct fusion allocation remains unresolved. This arrangement is a research candidate, not installed equipment.
